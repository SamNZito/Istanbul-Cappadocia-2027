Three Things The Data Says

1 · English is the trap

Highest pick rate in the game at 12.6%, third-worst win rate at 47.2%. It's the tutorial-friendly civ everyone starts on — longbows, simple economy, no dynasties or wings or sacred sites to manage. Then they never leave, and they lose 53% of their games doing it. House of Lancaster is the same civ with an extra 2 points, and almost nobody picks it.

2 · Variants have broken the parent civs

Every single variant civ that exists outperforms its parent, and some by a lot:

  • Byzantines 47.6% → Macedonian Dynasty 55.0% — a 7.4-point gap, the largest in the game. The worst-but-four civ has the best variant.
  • Chinese 44.9% → Zhu Xi's 52.0% / Jin 51.2% — the worst civ in the game is strictly dominated by both of its own children.
  • Mongols 48.7% → Golden Horde 53.7% — five full points.
  • HRE 47.8% → Order of the Dragon 52.5%
  • The lone exception: Japanese 51.5% → Sengoku Daimyo 46.3%. The only variant that's meaningfully worse than its parent, by 5.2 points.

3 · Read this table with one caveat

This is all-rank data across 889,689 games. Civs with high mechanical demands get dragged down by the bronze-to-gold bulk. Mongols and Chinese both perform substantially better at Conqueror level than these numbers suggest — their skill floor is punishing but their ceiling is high. Conversely, English and French look better here than they do at the top of the ladder. Filter by rank on AoE4World if you want the version that matches how you actually play.

Turkey — three civs, including the best one in the game

This is not a stretch. It's the same city.

  • Byzantines. Constantinople is Istanbul. The Hagia Sophia on your day-two itinerary is the Byzantine imperial cathedral, completed in 537. You would be standing inside the civ's landmark.
  • Macedonian Dynasty. The #1 civ in the game at 55.0% is a Byzantine variant named for the dynasty that ruled from Constantinople between 867 and 1056. The best faction in Age of Empires IV is a hotel walk from your Karaköy bar crawl.
  • Ottomans. Mehmed II took Constantinople in 1453 and moved the capital there. Topkapı Palace — also on your itinerary — is where the sultans actually lived. The Grand Bazaar opened eight years after the conquest.
  • Bonus: Cappadocia's cave churches are Byzantine. Those frescoes at the Göreme Open-Air Museum were painted by the civ.

Japan — two civs, the best and nearly the worst

  • Japanese (51.5%, best non-variant civ in the game). Kyoto was the imperial capital for over a thousand years. Three nights of your itinerary are in it.
  • Sengoku Daimyo (46.3%, second worst). The Sengoku period warlords. Osaka Castle — walkable from Dotonbori — was Toyotomi Hideyoshi's seat, and he's the definitive Sengoku daimyo.

South Korea and Thailand — zero

There is no Korean civilization in Age of Empires IV. Goryeo and Joseon are both absent despite Goryeo being a natural fit — it held off six Mongol invasions in the exact century the game covers. Closest adjacent civs are the Mongols (who invaded it) and the Jin Dynasty (who bordered it).

There is no Thai civilization either. Ayutthaya and Sukhothai are both missing, which is a real omission — you'd be walking the Ayutthaya ruins on your Bangkok day trip with no in-game counterpart to point at. Southeast Asia is the largest unrepresented region in the game.

Verdict, with the new column applied

On money and nightlife, nothing changed — South Korea is still the value pick at $2,945, and it has the best nights of the four. It also has zero representation in the game, which is a real omission on Relic's part and not much of a travel argument.

But if the tiebreaker is which trip lets you walk inside a civilization: Turkey wins outright and it isn't close. Three civs, one city. You'd have coffee under the dome of the Byzantine landmark, walk to the Ottoman palace, and the whole time you're inside the territory of the single best-performing faction in Age of Empires IV. Then you'd get in a balloon over Byzantine cave churches. No other destination on this list can offer a 55% win rate and a hot air balloon.
