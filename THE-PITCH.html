South Korea

Seoul 6 nights · Busan 2 nights · 10 days door to door
$2,945
$224/night ex-flight

The best nightlife in Asia bolted onto the best late-night food in Asia.

Nights

Three separate scenes, and you can do all three. Hongdae is young and indie — live music venues, craft cocktail bars, street performers until 4am, club entry around ₩15,000. Itaewon is international and the most English-friendly. Gangnam is the money tier, big rooms, tables from ₩300,000. Korean BBQ and soju stretches for hours and is the social center of the entire country. Then noraebang — private karaoke rooms, everywhere, all night.

Reference prices: convenience store beer ₩2,000–4,000, pint at a bar ₩6,000–10,000, soju ₩4,000–5,000, cocktails ₩15,000–25,000.

Days

The DMZ — the Joint Security Area, standing a hundred yards from North Korea. One of the strangest places on earth and a half-day from the city. Gwangjang Market for mung bean pancakes, hand-cut noodles, and live octopus if someone's feeling brave. Gyeongbokgung Palace — free entry if you rent a hanbok. Chimaek by the Han River, fried chicken and beer delivered to you on the grass. A Korean baseball game, which has organized chants for every player and is more fun than the sport warrants. And jjimjilbang — a 24-hour bathhouse with hot rooms, cold plunges, and heated sleeping floors. Ten dollars. Best hangover infrastructure on the planet.

Turkey

Istanbul 5 nights · Cappadocia 2 nights · 9 days door to door
$3,290
$343/night ex-flight

Two continents, three empires, and the best stories you'll bring home.

Nights

Meyhane night at a place like Asmalı Cavit — rakı, endless meze, live saz, tables that get louder over four hours. Ocakbaşı grills where you sit around an open coal pit and they cook in front of you, about $20 a head. Rooftops over the Bosphorus — 360 Istanbul, 16 Roof, Mikla. Karaköy for cocktail bars, Kadıköy on the Asian side for where locals actually drink, Ortaköy waterfront for the bottle-service version. Dress code is enforced on men — collared shirt, real shoes.

Days

Sunrise balloon over Cappadocia — hundreds launching at once over eroded volcanic spires, and October has the lowest cancellation rate of the year at 7–17%. A hammam built in 1580: steam room, a full-body scrub with a coarse mitt, foam massage on hot marble. Sixty to ninety dollars and the best jet-lag cure that exists. A private Bosphorus boat, $65 a head. Hagia Sophia, 1,500 years old. Derinkuyu, an underground city eight levels deep that sheltered 20,000 people. And a one-dollar ferry between two continents with tea service on board.

Thailand

Bangkok 4N · Chiang Mai 3N · Krabi 3N · 12 days door to door
$3,460
$235/night ex-flight

Everything costs nothing, and the food is a religion.

Nights

Ringside Muay Thai at Rajadamnern, 2,500 THB (~$75) — live band, betting section losing its mind, the real thing rather than a tourist show. Yaowarat Road in Chinatown after dark, arguably the best eating street on the planet: grilled seafood, crab omelettes, boat noodles, all from carts, all under $5. Rooftops at Sky Bar and Vertigo, real bars in Thonglor and Ekkamai, chaos on Khao San, clubs at RCA. Beer costs $2–4.

Days

Ethical elephant sanctuary outside Chiang Mai — no riding, you feed them by hand, ~$70. The Monk's Trail, a jungle path marked with orange cloth leading to a temple built into a waterfall. Monk Chat, where young monks sit down to practice English and ask you as many questions as you ask them. Private longtail charter in Krabi, whole boat all day, $45 each. Rock climbing at Railay on world-famous limestone, $35 for a half-day beginner course. Grand Palace, Wat Pho, Ayutthaya ruins. And hour-long massages for $10, everywhere, all trip.

Japan

Tokyo 4N · Kyoto 3N · Osaka 2N · 11 days door to door
$3,525
$258/night ex-flight

Everything works, nothing disappoints, and the nights get strange in the best way.

Nights

Golden Gai — six narrow alleys in Shinjuku holding about 200 bars, most of them seating six people, each with its own theme and its own master. You bar-hop by climbing tiny staircases. There is nothing else like it anywhere. Omoide Yokocho next to Shinjuku Station: smoke-filled yakitori counters in alleys barely wide enough for two. Private karaoke rooms all night with drinks delivered. Standing bars in Osaka's Temma and Kyobashi — gritty, local, cheap, the best drinking in the country. Dotonbori at midnight under the neon.

Days

Fushimi Inari at 6am — 10,000 vermilion gates up a mountainside, completely empty if you beat the crowd. Bikes through Kyoto, which is flat and made for it. Shibuya Sky over the crossing. teamLab. Hakone for onsen with Fuji views, or Nikko, which has real autumn color by late October. Japanese baseball, which has brass sections and is completely unhinged. One kaiseki dinner, a dozen tiny seasonal courses, $120–180. And the food floor everywhere else is higher than most countries' ceiling — a $9 bowl of ramen beats a $60 dinner in most cities.

The number most people miss: total price is misleading because the trips are different lengths and the flight is a fixed lump. My recommendation

South Korea. Ten days. $2,945. Cheapest total on the board, best nightlife of the four, peak season in October, no visa, and it costs two fewer days off work than Thailand and one less than Japan.

You opened this asking for somewhere the dollar goes far with good nights. Korea is the only one of the four that's simultaneously the cheapest and the best at the thing you said matters most. If the group will stretch to 13 days and four grand, put Tokyo and Kyoto on the front of it — the Seoul hop is two and a half hours and under $215 round trip.
