The Shape Of It

Istanbul5 nights at Soho House, Beyoğlu — restored 19th-century palazzo, rooftop over the Bosphorus, pool, spa, bar downstairs, and walking distance to the Karaköy nightlife.
Cappadocia2 nights at Kayakapi Premium Caves, Ürgüp — rock-cut rooms with panoramic valley views.
HistoricalHagia Sophia, standing since 537. Topkapı Palace. The Basilica Cistern. An underground city eight levels deep. Byzantine cave churches.
NightsClubs on the water at Ortaköy. Rooftops over the Bosphorus. A bar street on the Asian side where nobody's a tourist.
FoodA Michelin-starred dinner, a grill feast around an open coal pit for $20, and a four-hour rakı-and-meze night.
The one thingA hot air balloon at sunrise over a valley of volcanic spires. Balloons fly roughly 90% of May mornings.
