The Two Worth Taking Seriously

Uzbekistan — the best-value trip on any list I've built you

Three things landed at once here, and the first one is brand new:

  • Visa-free for Americans since 1 January 2026. No application, no fee, no e-visa. 30 days on a valid passport.
  • State Department Level 1 — Exercise Normal Precautions. The lowest tier that exists. Nothing else on any list I've given you is Level 1. Not Japan's neighbors, not Turkey, not Thailand.
  • September and October is the window, and it's closing — the country is on track for 12 million visitors this year.

What it is: the Silk Road, intact. The Registan in Samarkand is three madrasas facing a plaza, tiled floor to dome in blue. Bukhara is a walled old city that's been continuously inhabited for two thousand years. Khiva is a preserved desert fortress. Genghis Khan sacked all of it in 1220 — that's your Mongol connection — and Timur rebuilt Samarkand into the capital of an empire.

Trains between cities cost $24–39. High-speed, modern, book on 12go.asia since international cards often fail on the official rail site.

Egypt — the closest thing to a real challenger

Pound at 50.25 to the dollar after a full collapse. Level 2 — the same tier as France, Italy, and the UK — with Do Not Travel confined to North/Middle Sinai and parts of the Western Desert, nowhere near Cairo, Luxor, or Aswan. October is the start of peak season. Roughly 11 hours nonstop from JFK on EgyptAir.

What it is: Pyramids and the Sphinx. The Grand Egyptian Museum. A four-night Nile cruise from Luxor to Aswan — 5-star boats start at $490 per person for four nights, all meals included. Valley of the Kings. Abu Simbel. Saladin's Citadel over Cairo, which is your literal Ayyubid landmark.

No. None of the civ homelands beat Japan, Korea, Turkey, or Thailand for this specific trip — and the reason is the same every time. You said the priority is nights out. Every AoE4 homeland that survives the safety screen is a history-and-scenery country with quiet evenings. They'd be spectacular trips. They wouldn't be your trip.

But file Uzbekistan. Visa-free as of January, the only Level 1 advisory on any list I've handed you, $2,140 for ten days, and $128 a night on the ground — the cheapest number in this entire project by a wide margin. It's the best-value trip I've found for you.
