Flying from three different cities is fine — here's how to handle it

  • Book separately. No reason to be on the same plane. Everyone gets the best fare from their own airport.
  • Coordinate arrival windows, not flights. Aim to land within about four hours of each other on Friday 21 May. Turkish Airlines' evening departures from all three cities land mid-to-late afternoon Istanbul time, so this works naturally.
  • Whoever lands first checks in and holds both rooms. Soho House will store bags for early arrivals.
  • Everyone books their own airport transfer or takes the M11 metro. Don't try to sync a shared van across three arrival times.
  • Set your own fare alert for your own airport. The Boston guy shouldn't wait on the New York guy's price.

What the lean tier actually gives up

You still get

  • The sunrise balloon over Cappadocia
  • Two nights in a real cave hotel
  • Hagia Sophia and the Basilica Cistern
  • Blue Mosque, Süleymaniye, both bazaars, Balat — all free anyway
  • Every night out. Bars and clubs are priced the same for everyone
  • Ocakbaşı grill feasts and a full meyhane night
  • The ferry between continents. It's a dollar
  • An underground city and Göreme's cave churches

You give up

  • Topkapı Palace — €55, the single most expensive ticket in the city
  • The private Bosphorus charter — you take the public ferry instead, which is genuinely 80% of the experience for 3% of the price
  • The 1580 hammam — a neighborhood hamam is $25–30 instead of $85 and the scrub is the same
  • ATVs in the valleys
  • The Michelin dinner
  • Hotel quality — 3-star boutique rather than a palazzo. Clean, central, small rooms

The Important Part: What's Shared vs Individual

You do not all have to pick the same tier. Only one line item genuinely requires a group decision — everything else, each person can flex on privately without anyone else noticing or caring.

The practical recommendation

Pick the middle hotel tier as a group — that's about $695 per person for all seven nights — and then let everyone flex individually on food, drinks, and optional activities.

That structure lets the guy who's tight land around $2,400 and the guy who wants everything land around $3,600, on the exact same trip, sleeping in the same hotel, out at the same bars every night. Nobody has to announce their budget and nobody misses anything that matters.
